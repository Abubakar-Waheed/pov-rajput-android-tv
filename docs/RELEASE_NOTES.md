# POV Rajput 0.2.17

## Playback

- New **Read-ahead** setting in **Settings → Playback**: Standard (50 seconds), Long (2 minutes, the new default) or Maximum (5 minutes). The player downloads further ahead so slow or uneven connections are less likely to stall.
- The buffer can use up to half of the app's memory, which the app may now raise to Android's large-memory limit. On a 2 GB box like the onn 4K that is about 190 MB: around 25 seconds of a 4K remux, or one and a half to two minutes of a typical 1080p file.
